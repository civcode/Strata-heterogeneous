## Independent 256K sizing (analytical, not admission)

Added `tools/heterogeneous_q2_capacity.py`, a CPU-only conservative component/subtotal estimator for the measured 0039 2K, max_t=8 Q2 geometry, 1 GiB reserve and resident placements. Its formulas follow `session_bytes`, `qsa_state_bytes`, `kv_plan` and `qsa_buffers_bytes`: per QSA layer/cell, INT8 codes 1024 B plus FP16 scales 32 B, or F16 K/V 2048 B; indexer pooled rows 128 B/cell and page table 1 B/cell. One shared RoPE table adds 256 B/cell per owner, and shared cell-score workspace adds 4 B/cell. GDN recurrence/conv state, QSA fixed/index tails, PLE history, MoE/block/shared fixed workspaces and alignment are included in the measured 2K session anchor. The estimator accepts only 256-cell-aligned contexts, preserving the native alignment increment. It reproduces all three observed mixed 2K F16/INT8 session arenas and both HIP INT8 arenas; HIP F16 uses a conservative 512-byte alignment allowance rather than claiming an unmeasured arena.

At 262144 cells the analytical INT8 **session** bytes are mixed `1048566272 / 1673123328 / 1355941888` and HIP `2000110592 / 2000110592`. Adding the measured pack-weight arena, full expert-slot allocation and configured reserve gives mixed `15097017344 / 18115360640 / 15484656256` and HIP `23085058816 / 23176276736` bytes. F16 mixed subtotals are `15877157632 / 19415594624 / 16524843392`; HIP conservative subtotals `24645339392 / 24736557312`. These are **incomplete lower bounds, not peak VRAM, fit predictions or 256K qualification**. Separately allocated native GGUF dense weights, entry-stage PLE projection/scratch and table/host I/O, native verifier/graph allocations, staging/transport, prefill buffers, runtime/library allocations and temporaries are excluded. Native-stage sizing explicitly calls `NativeDense::weight_bytes_for` and adds entry-stage PLE device scratch; verify/prefill lifetimes and temporary peaks must be accounted for independently, and measured free/resident/peak headroom must be established. The fixed measured pack and expert allocations cannot substitute for those missing terms. No cap was raised and no long-context GPU allocation was attempted.
