Native `draft_first` copies the row to bound `window_R_` using a default-stream D2D `cudaMemcpy`, then the native round graph consumes that buffer on its own stream; the original front trace observed the result after the round's `copy_from_mapped_multi` to `Rin_`.

This directly observes the source update overlapping round execution, even though the additional taps perturbed timing and masked the final failure in that run. It localizes the earliest observed wrong operation to the cross-stream source staging boundary, not a stale committed KV or a demonstrated native math defect. The graph taps are samples, not an atomic snapshot of a racing write; they do not prove exactly which runtime scheduling/cache behavior caused the overlap.
