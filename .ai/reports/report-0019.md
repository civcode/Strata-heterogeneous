## Trace correction

Initial live snapshots exposed a diagnostic bug, not a draft fix: native BF16 K/V pool layout is `[page][kv_head][cell][head_dim]` (see `src/kernels/cuda/qsa.cu`), whereas the v1 trace incorrectly trimmed the physical bytes as though cells were contiguous. Thus v1 per-cell comparisons and apparent differences are invalid. Trace schema **v2** deinterleaves complete cells across heads from native `conversation_kv_save(include_index=false)`; unsupported non-BF16 layouts fail closed. Comparator rejects v1, with a regression fixture. Snapshots remain synchronous, fully resident, and trimmed to committed cells; no future suffix is retained. The v2 CPU comparator tests do not independently exercise GPU layout extraction; source layout and live byte comparisons are the present evidence.
